# EU EPR Registration — Germany & France

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照各国官方登记处核验。计划范围：仅德国与法国。

## 为什么是德法
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is enforced nationally. For cross-border sellers the two markets with the hardest marketplace enforcement are Germany (VerpackG market ban, WEEE number required) and France (Unique Identifier on invoices). Amazon/eBay block listings that lack the registration numbers.

## 德国（三项注册）
1. **VerpackG（包装法）**: register with **LUCID** (Verpackungsregister) before selling ANY packaged goods (including the product box and shipping carton) and join a dual system (e.g. Grüne Punkt, Interseroh) for recycling financing, reporting volumes annually. Selling without LUCID number = **market ban (Marktsperre)** — marketplaces must delist.
2. **ElektroG（WEEE）**: register with **stiftung ear**, get the WEEE-Reg.-Nr. (DE 7-digit); appoint an authorized representative if not EU-established; report volumes.
3. **BattG（电池法）**: register batteries (BEV register) if selling devices with or containing batteries; battery take-back financing.

## 法国（IDU 责任人识别码）
France requires an **Identifiant Unique (IDU)** — the producer's EPR registration number issued via the ADEME producer portal — for each EPR scheme the product falls under:
- **Électronique/EEE** (scheme: e.g. Ecosystem or Ecologic)
- **Piles/batteries** (scheme: e.g. Screlec)
- **Emballages** (scheme: e.g. Citeo)
The IDU must be communicated to marketplaces and appear on invoices; marketplaces must verify it before allowing sales (article L. 541-10-13 Code de l'environnement — "duty of vigilance").

## 注册前置条件
- Neither country accepts registrations without an EU-established entity or appointed **authorized representative** (Bevollmächtigter / représentant) — Chinese sellers typically use AR service providers (fees ~€100-500/year per register).
- Fees: Germany LUCID registration is free (dual system costs by volume); stiftung ear registration has annual fees by device type; French schemes have minimum annual contributions (~€100-300 per scheme for small volumes).

## 实操顺序（新卖家清单）
1. 先定 AR（德法可同一机构）
2. 德国：LUCID → 双元系统 → stiftung ear →（含电池）BEV
3. 法国：ADEME 门户注册 → 选择 eco-organisme → 拿 IDU → 报给 Amazon
4. 把注册号回填平台后台（Amazon Seller Central 的合规中心会逐项检查）
