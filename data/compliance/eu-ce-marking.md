# EU CE Marking Essentials

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 European Commission 官网核验。

## CE 标志是什么
CE marking declares that a product placed on the EU/EEA market complies with all applicable EU legislation (directives/regulations) directing it. It is a manufacturer's self-declaration for most product groups — not a quality mark or a certificate. Responsible person: the **manufacturer established in the EU**, or an **authorised representative** / for distance selling the **importer or fulfilment service provider** designated in writing (per the Market Surveillance Regulation (EU) 2019/1020).

## 哪些产品需要 CE
Product groups with CE-marking legislation include: toys (Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC), electronics (RoHS, EMC, Low Voltage Directive, Radio Equipment Directive), machinery, personal protective equipment (PPE), medical devices, construction products, pressure equipment, recreational craft. **Products not covered by any CE legislation must NOT be CE marked** (e.g. most furniture, packaging) — incorrectly affixing CE is itself a violation.

## 制造商合规步骤
1. Identify all applicable EU legislation (often several directives apply at once, e.g. RED + RoHS + EMC for a Bluetooth speaker).
2. Apply harmonized standards (listed in the Official Journal) — conformity to harmonized standards creates a **presumption of conformity** but is voluntary.
3. Perform the required **conformity assessment procedure**: internal production control (Module A, self-declaration) for most consumer directives; **notified body involvement** for higher-risk categories (e.g. certain PPE, medical devices, toys under EN 71 part where chemical testing by notified body can be required... note: toys normally Module A but with third-party chemical testing options per the directive).
4. Draw up the **Declaration of Conformity (DoC)** — must name the product, the manufacturer, the applied directives/standards, and be kept **10 years** after the last unit is placed on the market.
5. Compile and keep the **technical file** (design, risk assessments, test reports) — also 10 years.
6. Affix the CE mark visibly, legibly and indelibly on the product or, if impossible, on packaging and accompanying documents.

## 进口商与经销商义务
Importers (established in EU, placing product from a non-EU manufacturer on the EU market) must verify the manufacturer's DoC and technical documentation, keep a register of complaints/non-conformities, and ensure storage/transport don't jeopardise compliance. Importer name and address must appear on the product. Distributors must verify CE marking and DoC accompany the product.

## 英国脱欧注意
CE marking is no longer valid alone for Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales): the **UKCA mark** applies for most of the same product groups, with the UK's own designated standards; Northern Ireland accepts CE (and UKNI). Practical note: many sellers maintain both CE and UKCA files.
