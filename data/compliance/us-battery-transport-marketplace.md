# US Lithium Battery Rules & Product Safety for Camera Gear

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 FAA/DOT/CPSC 官网核验。

## 运输规则（DOT 49 CFR / IATA）
- Lithium-ion batteries are Class 9 hazardous materials in transport (49 CFR 173.185).
- **UN 3481** (batteries contained in/packed with equipment) shipments need: batteries type-tested to **UN 38.3**, terminals protected, and the lithium battery mark on the package.
- Shipments from overseas sellers by air follow IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations — many logistics providers require the **UN 38.3 test summary** and (for standalone batteries) IEC 62133-2 / UL 2054 / UL 1642 test reports as a condition of carriage.
- USPS/FedEx/UPS each have lithium battery mailing programs with size/quantity rules — mis-declared battery parcels are the top cause of customs seizures in this category.

## 消费品安全（CPSC）
- No mandatory federal certification for camera equipment itself; CPSC can recall unsafe products under the Consumer Product Safety Act / FHSA (fire & burn hazards from batteries are the recalled category — hoverboards, power banks precedent).
- Voluntary standards the market expects: **UL 2054 / IEC 62133-2** (lithium cells/packs), **UL 62368-1** (AV/ICT equipment safety, superseding UL 60950-1/60065).
- Chargers/mains adapters: no federal NRTL mandate, but retailers and insurers effectively require UL/ETL listing.

## 随身携带（差旅场景）
FAA rules passengers must know (also good FAQ content for sellers):
- Spare lithium-ion batteries: **carry-on only**, never checked; ≤100 Wh each, 100–160 Wh limited to 2 spares with airline approval; terminals taped or in original packaging.
- Batteries installed in devices may go in checked or carry-on.
- Damaged or recalled batteries are forbidden on aircraft.

## 平台实操（Amazon US）
Amazon requires compliance documentation for battery-containing listings and has pulled categories until documents are provided:
- Rechargeable lithium battery products: **UN 38.3 test summary + MSDS/SDS + IEC 62133-2 or UL 2054/1642 report**.
- Chargers/power adapters: NRTL (UL/ETL) report to UL 62368-1 or IEC 62368-1.
- RF devices: FCC ID or SDoC documentation (see FCC 文档).
