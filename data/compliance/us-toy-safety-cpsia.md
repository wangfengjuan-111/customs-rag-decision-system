# US Toy & Children's Product Safety (CPSIA)

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 CPSC 官网核验。

## 适用范围
Toys and other children's products imported or sold in the US are governed by the Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act (CPSIA, 2008) and the Consumer Product Safety Act, enforced by the CPSC. A "children's product" is a consumer product designed or intended primarily for children 12 years of age or younger — determined by manufacturer statements, packaging, marketing, and common recognition.

## 强制玩具标准 ASTM F963
The ASTM F963 toy safety standard is mandatory under CPSIA (15 U.S.C. § 2056b). It covers mechanical and physical hazards (small parts, sharp points and edges, strangulation hazards), flammability, acoustics, and soluble heavy metal migration limits for 8 elements (antimony, arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, selenium).

## 铅与邻苯二甲酸盐限量
- Total lead in substrate (accessible component parts): **100 ppm (0.01%)**.
- Lead in paint and surface coatings: **90 ppm (0.009%)**.
- Phthalates: **8 phthalates limited to 0.1% (1000 ppm)** each — DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP (toys and child care articles), plus DIBP, DPENP, DHEXP, DCHP. Child care articles = products for sleeping/feeding/sucking for children 3 and under.

## 第三方检测与认证
1. Testing must be performed by a **CPSC-accepted third-party laboratory** (accredited to ISO/IEC 17025).
2. Based on passing results, the manufacturer or **US importer** must issue a **Children's Product Certificate (CPC)** certifying compliance with each applicable rule (lead, phthalates, ASTM F963, etc.). The CPC must accompany the product or a shipment batch.
3. Periodic testing at least **once per year** (or per a periodic testing plan under 16 CFR Part 1107).
4. The importer is legally responsible for compliance if the foreign manufacturer has no US agent.

## 追踪标签
Permanent tracking labels are required on the product and packaging: manufacturer/private labeler, location and date of production, and batch/run number (15 U.S.C. § 2063(a)(5)).

## 小零件要求
Toys intended for children under 3 must not contain small parts (fit in the small-parts cylinder) — 16 CFR Part 1501. Also see 16 CFR 1500.18 for banned toys for toddlers. Marketing that avoids "under 3" claims does not override intended-use determination.
