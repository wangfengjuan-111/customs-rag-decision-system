# EU GPSR — General Product Safety Regulation

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 European Commission 官网核验。

## 适用范围
The GPSR (Regulation (EU) 2023/988) applies from **13 December 2024** to consumer products placed or made available on the EU market that are not covered by other EU harmonized safety legislation — and as a safety net it applies alongside sector-specific rules. It replaces the old General Product Safety Directive. It explicitly covers **online marketplaces and distance selling**, including products sold from outside the EU directly to EU consumers.

## 经济经营者责任
- **Manufacturer**: ensure products are safe, carry out internal risk assessments, keep technical documentation, and (new) ensure a **responsible economic operator established in the EU** exists.
- **Importers**: verify manufacturer compliance, do not place unsafe products on the market, keep registers.
- **Online marketplaces**: due-diligence obligations for traders; each product offered online must display the identity of the EU-established economic operator and, where applicable, product safety warnings.
- Key requirement for cross-border sellers: **no product without an EU-established responsible person** may be sold to EU consumers (this parallels the EU Market Surveillance Regulation).

## 产品安全要求
A safe product is one that, under normal or reasonably foreseeable use, does not present risk or only minimal risk. Safety assessment must consider: product characteristics, presentation, labelling, warnings, effect on other products, categories of users at risk (children, elderly). Products must be traceable (batch/serial numbers), and manufacturers must immediately notify authorities and recall where products are dangerous — with public recall notices (Safety Gate).

## 对跨境电商的实际影响
1. Selling on Amazon EU / EU direct-to-consumer requires naming an EU responsible person on the listing or product.
2. Risk assessment documentation should exist for every product before listing.
3. Safety Gate (the EU rapid alert system, formerly RAPEX) notifications can trigger marketplace delisting.
4. GPSR interacts with the EU Product Liability Directive (updated 2024): damages from unsafe products extend to software and online sales channels.
