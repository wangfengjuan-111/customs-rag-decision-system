# EU RoHS & REACH for Electronics and Accessories

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 ECHA 官网核验。

## RoHS 限制物质（EEE 适用）
RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU (recast) restricts in electrical & electronic equipment:
- Lead (Pb), mercury (Hg), hexavalent chromium (CrVI), PBB, PBDE — 0.1% each
- Cadmium — 0.01%
- Four phthalates (added by Directive (EU) 2015/863): DEHP, BBP, DBP, DIBP — 0.1% each
Applies to homogeneous materials. Scope = 11 EEE categories including cables and accessories (category 11 "other EEE"): a USB cable, charger, or electronic selfie stick is in scope; a purely mechanical tripod is not.

## REACH 义务
REACH (EC 1907/2006) applies to **all** articles, mechanical or electronic:
- **SVHC candidate list** (updated ~2×/year, 240+ substances): if an article contains ≥0.1% (w/w) SVHC, the supplier must communicate safe-use information down the chain — for consumer sales this means informing the recipient; ECHA's **SCIP database** notification is required for articles containing SVHCs placed on the EU market.
- **Annex XVII restrictions**: e.g. phthalates in plasticized materials, cadmium in plastics, lead in consumer articles (Reg. (EU) 2023/632 tightened lead limits), PAHs in rubber/plastic skin-contact parts.

## 电子产品常见风险点
- PVC cable insulation/jacket: lead stabilizers, DEHP, SCCPs
- Solder and finishes: lead (RoHS exemptions for some applications have lapsed)
- Housings and adhesives: phthalates, flame retardants (short-chain chlorinated paraffins banned)
- Surface coatings: nickel release (skin-contact metal parts), cadmium in paints
- Batteries inside accessories: separate Batteries Regulation rules apply (see WEEE/电池文档)

## 与 CE 的关系
RoHS is part of the CE compliance chain for EEE: the DoC must reference RoHS alongside EMC/LVD/RED. REACH applies independently of CE marking — even a non-electronic accessory (lens protector, strap, mount) must comply with REACH and can trigger SVHC communication duties.
