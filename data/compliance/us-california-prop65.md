# California Proposition 65 for Electronics & Accessories

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照 OEHHA 官网核验。

## 是什么
Prop 65 (Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act of 1986) requires a **"clear and reasonable" warning** before knowingly and intentionally exposing anyone in California to a listed chemical (list of ~900+, updated regularly). It is a warning statute, not a product ban — but private plaintiffs can enforce it with penalties (historically $2,500/day/violation) making it the most-litigated consumer compliance risk for Chinese sellers in the US.

## 电子产品与配件的高频触发物
- **Lead** — PVC cable jackets, solder, metal alloys/finishes (brass, zinc-plated parts)
- **Phthalates (DEHP, DBP, DINP...)** — flexible PVC, cable insulation, soft-grip coatings, adhesives
- **Bisphenol A (BPA)** — polycarbonate parts, epoxy coatings
- **Nickel** — skin-contact metal parts (watch straps, mounts)
- **Carbon black, titanium dioxide** — black plastic housings, painted parts
Selfie sticks (rubized grips, metal finish), lens protectors (adhesives), protective cases (TPU coatings), camera straps and mounts are commonly flagged in settlements.

## 警告标签要求
- **Short-form warning** (allowed since August 2018, product label): `⚠ WARNING: Cancer and Reproductive Harm — www.P65Warnings.ca.gov` (for exposures to both carcinogens and reproductive toxicants).
- For consumer product exposures the warning must appear on the product label or shelf tag; for internet purchases it must also appear on the **website display page** (Amazon requires sellers to provide Prop 65 warnings for flagged listings — non-compliant listings get suppressed).
- "Knowingly and intentionally" exposure: businesses with <10 employees are exempt; below safe-harbor levels (Maximum Allowable Dose Levels) no warning is needed — proving safe-harbor compliance usually needs exposure analysis.

## 实操建议
1. Ask suppliers for Prop 65-compliant materials or third-party testing (CPSC-accepted or accredited labs) for lead/phthalates on all PVC, coated, and metal parts.
2. If formulation control is uncertain, affix the short-form warning — it's cheap insurance and stops the "failure to warn" claim.
3. Check the OEHHA list for the specific substances in your materials; settlements in this space average low five figures plus reformulation.
