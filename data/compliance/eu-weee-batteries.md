# EU WEEE, Batteries Regulation & Lithium Battery Transport

> ⚠️ draft：内容按法规公共知识整理，正式使用前必须对照官方来源核验。

## WEEE（电子电器废弃物）
Directive 2012/19/EU, transposed nationally:
- Every EEE (cameras, gimbals, chargers — "small equipment" category) placed on an EU market requires **producer registration in that member state** (national registers, e.g. stiftung ear in Germany) and financing of collection/recycling.
- Cross-border/distance sellers must register in **each** country of sale or appoint an authorized representative.
- Products must bear the **crossed-out wheelie bin symbol** (separate collection marking).
- Marketplace listings for Germany require a WEEE registration number — Amazon enforces this (blocked listings without it).

## EU 电池法规 (EU) 2023/1542
Replaced the old Batteries Directive, phased in from 2024:
- **CE marking for batteries** became mandatory (transition from 18 August 2024).
- Portable batteries in devices must be **removable and replaceable by end users** (from February 2027 for devices placed on market).
- Labelling: chemistry (Li-ion), capacity, separate collection symbol, and the **"CE" mark** on the battery.
- Built-in battery products (cameras): the device must be designed for battery removal with commonly available tools — non-compliant designs cannot be placed on the EU market.
- Removability applies with exemptions (devices needing continuity e.g. medical), consumer cameras are expected to comply.

## 锂电池航空运输（卖家的货从中国发货适用）
Lithium-ion batteries are **Class 9 dangerous goods**:
- **UN 3480** — batteries shipped alone; **UN 3481** — batteries packed with equipment or contained in equipment (an action camera with its battery = UN 3481, PI 967; spare battery in the same box = PI 966).
- Cells/batteries must be type-tested to **UN 38.3** (T1–T8 tests); a **test summary** from the manufacturer is a shipping document requirement.
- By air: lithium-ion batteries alone (UN 3480) must ship at ≤30% state of charge, cargo aircraft only; equipment-containing batteries (UN 3481) can go on passenger aircraft under quantity/cell limits.
- **100 Wh threshold**: batteries ≤100 Wh are "small" (simpler rules, quantity limits per package); 100–160 Wh need operator approval; >160 Wh are fully regulated cargo. Action-camera batteries (~5–20 Wh) are well below 100 Wh.
- Packages with lithium batteries must carry the **UN 3481 lithium battery mark**; cells must be protected against short circuit.
